Our Mission

To gather the community upon the teachings of Ahle Sunnat, strengthen Islamic brotherhood, and keep hearts connected to deen with adab and sincerity.

Our Vision

To provide a welcoming and beneficial home where people of all ages can stay connected with Ahle Sunnat programs and values.

Our Spirit

We encourage compassion, practical service, and unity through collective effort for the betterment of the local Muslim community.
