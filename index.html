Ahle Sunnat London

A community devoted to remembrance of Allah جل جلاله, love for the Beloved Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and sincere service for the local Muslim community of London, Ontario.

Support Our Work View Programmes Check Namaz Timings

Our Mission

To preserve authentic Ahle Sunnat teachings, strengthen spiritual practice, and build a respectful environment where families and youth stay connected to deen.

Our Vision

To serve as a trusted home for beneficial Islamic gatherings, unity, and organized community support in London.

Our Commitment

We value adab, sincerity, and practical action, keeping every effort focused on beneficial outcomes for the community.

Services & Activities

How We Serve The Community

Our work combines spiritual nourishment, education, and service-based initiatives to strengthen community wellbeing.

01

Faith Gatherings

Regular majalis and reminders focused on Qur'an, Sunnah, and character building.

02

Programme Hosting

Organized events for Ramadan, Milad, and community occasions with inclusive participation.

03

Prayer Awareness

Daily timing tools and monthly date support for better worship planning at home and in the masjid.

04

Community Support

Collective volunteer-based efforts that prioritize compassion, transparency, and trust.

Community Values

  • Respectful communication and sincere intentions in every effort.
  • Consistency in worship, learning, and service to one another.
  • Balanced leadership rooted in trust, humility, and accountability.
